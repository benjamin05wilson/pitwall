# Pitwall — modelling decisions & limitations

The reasoning behind the engine's modelling and engineering choices, and an honest register of what it does *not* yet do. The limitations section is deliberately the longest: knowing exactly where the model is weak — and saying so before being asked — is the point.


## Limitations to volunteer first

The handful a sharp reviewer *will* find. Raise them yourself, framed as "here's the boundary and here's what would move it":


### Temperature → degradation coupling is built but shipped **inert** (`temp_coeff = 0.0`).

- **Why it's acceptable:** The two-stage S/M/H fit returned a wrong-signed slope (r = -0.55 over 45 points). Shipping a wrong-signed coefficient is strictly worse than zero — it would invert reality (steepen wear on cool tracks). The plumbing is live and native-consistent, so reactivation is a one-line swap, not a rebuild.
- **What would fix it:** Pirelli per-compound construction data + tyre-surface-temperature telemetry; fit within a single construction year rather than pooling across Pirelli re-specs.


### The SC/VSC cheap-stop discount is the **published prior** (0.50 / 0.35), not a data fit.

- **Why it's acceptable:** `PitOut - PitIn` measures pit-lane transit time, which barely changes under a safety car; the time *saved* is time the field loses crawling, which that telemetry can't see. So the quantity is computed but honestly not shipped — it isn't the estimand. This is the single biggest unverified lever on undercut/overcut value, and I flag it as such.
- **What would fix it:** Field-synchronous gap reconstruction (all 20 cars on a common clock), or a ghost-style sim estimate of time-lost-to-field under SC vs green, validated on real neutralised stops.


### The Norris/Bahrain `+7` headline is partly a **broken-real-car artefact**.

- **Why it's acceptable:** His real race was a mechanical 6-stop, so the gain mixes 'remove the failure' with 'better strategy'. The clean pure-strategy gains (the safety-car cases) are smaller — 1 to 3 places — and those are the honest demo. The ghost reports the sim finish *and* the actual finish so the comparison is transparent.
- **What would fix it:** Headline the clean cases; keep Norris as an extreme illustration only.


### The overtaking / track-position model still uses **uncalibrated 2019 TUM constants** (`p_overtake = 0.25`, thresholds).

- **Why it's acceptable:** Everything *strategy-facing* is calibrated (deg, pace, pit loss, SC probability, dirty air, wet pace); the overtaking layer is the part still on literature priors. It affects how cleanly track-position trades resolve, not the core stint maths.
- **What would fix it:** A logistic P(pass | pace_delta, gap, circuit) fit from position+gap+DRS telemetry — a clean sklearn/PyTorch surrogate.


### Wet calibration rests on **2 races / 31 full-wet laps** (the intermediate floor is firmer at 471 laps).

- **Why it's acceptable:** Each race's own dry laps cancel circuit/setup, and the *ordering* validates externally — the wet DP calls Verstappen's real inter switch to the lap at Zandvoort (L62) and Monaco (L56). Sample sizes are surfaced (`n_wet = 31`) so the magnitude is never over-trusted.
- **What would fix it:** Accumulate future full-wet races; weight the full-wet floor toward the better-sampled intermediate floor via the physical bowl until more data exists.


### Dirty-air partial r-values are **modest (~0.13–0.29)**.

- **Why it's acceptable:** Wake depends on unobserved aero setup and driver inputs (steering, lift-and-coast, DRS), so low r is expected. What matters for strategy is the sign and the cross-circuit ordering, and both are physically correct (Monaco 0.83 ≫ Monza 0.13) on large held-lap counts.
- **What would fix it:** Team CFD wake maps, or telemetry features (throttle/brake/DRS/lateral position) to lift the explained variance.


### No formal **confidence intervals** on the calibrated priors; the sim carries a few-positions **absolute** error.

- **Why it's acceptable:** That's why the ghost reports the real-vs-AI *delta* under common random numbers as the apples-to-apples quantity, not the absolute sim finish. Backtest Spearman is ~0.72 — good for ordering, not a claim of point precision.
- **What would fix it:** Bootstrap/Bayesian CIs on each calibrated parameter; report them in `priors.json` alongside n.

## Key decisions, by area


### STATISTICS & CALIBRATION

- Identify per-compound degradation with a single fixed-effects regression on fuel-corrected lap times: per-driver intercepts (per-race-and-driver in the pooled fit) absorb car/driver pace, a per-race linear track-evolution term absorbs rubbering-in, and the k0 offsets / k1 slopes are then identified purely from within-driver variation as a tyre ages. This is the classic guard against the FIA two-compound rule, which forces different drivers onto different compound sequences and would otherwise confound the tyre slope with driver pace.
- Two layers of robustness, attacking different failure modes: a per-(driver,compound) median pre-filter keeps only laps <= 1.07x that group's median (kills SC/traffic-tainted slow laps before the fit), then 4 IRLS-Huber iterations with k=1.345 on a MAD scale down-weight the moderate residual outliers that survive. The pre-filter is one-sided (slow laps only); Huber is two-sided on residuals.
- Prefer the pooled fit (fit_compounds_pooled) over single-race: shared per-compound k0/k1 but per-(race,driver) fixed effects and per-race evolution. Pooling 3 years per circuit gives the compound overlap that pins slopes a single race leaves confounded. Capped at 3 recent years (2023-25) to bound Pirelli construction drift; build_priors regenerates on demand so drift is detectable year-on-year.
- SC/VSC cheap-stop discount: calibrate_pit_loss DOES compute a telemetry fraction (1 - median(neutralised pit_loss)/median(green pit_loss)), but build_circuit deliberately does NOT ship it, because PitOut-PitIn measures pit-lane transit time, not time lost relative to a slowed field. The shipped model keeps the published prior (sc_saving_frac=0.50, vsc_saving_frac=0.35). Honest non-ship of a computed-but-unidentified quantity, not an un-attempted one.
- Temperature->degradation coupling: fully built (per-lap weather join, two-stage estimator, native-consistent slope() path) but shipped inert at temp_coeff=0.0. The S/M/H-aggregated two-stage fit returned r=-0.553 over 45 points (wrong-signed vs the physical prior that hotter rubber wears faster). build_temp_coupling holds any non-positive coefficient at 0.0 and logs it. Reactivates with Pirelli per-compound construction data or tyre-surface temps.
- Shrink each circuit's empirical P(>=1 SC) toward its circuit-profile prior with a Bayesian conjugate update, K=4 pseudo-observations: p = (p_emp*n + p_prior*K)/(n+K), with n~3 real races. Ship both raw and shrunk (p_sc_raw, p_sc) so a strategist sees the data signal. K=4 modestly out-weights ~3 races; chosen for regularisation, not tuned to a metric.
- Split mechanical DNF from incident/contact retirements using Jolpica status strings (_is_finish accepts Finished/Lapped/+N Lap; _INCIDENT_STATUSES excludes Accident/Collision/Spun off/DSQ/DNS etc.), so per-constructor p_dnf reflects the car, not the driver. Field overall came out at 0.11; this feeds the Monte-Carlo finish distribution.
- Calibrate dirty-air wake penalty only on held-station laps (gap_ahead < 3.0 s AND gap not shrinking faster than 0.4 s/lap) so a fast car reeling in a slow one can't invert the sign. Regress fuel-corrected lap time on a proximity term max(0, gap0 - gap_ahead) alongside the same FE structure; ship only when theta>0 and n_held>=200. Result Monaco 0.83 s >> Monza 0.13 s, n_held 880-1500 per circuit.
- Calibrate wet pace from real wet races using each race's own dry slick laps as the baseline (circuit/setup cancel) and the 10th-percentile fuel-corrected lap per compound as best-case pace. Intermediate floor (9.23 s) rests on 471 inter laps; full-wet floor (18.54 s) on only 31 laps across 2 races - thin and reported as such (n_inter, n_wet, n_races). Bowl curvature stays a physical prior; live layer re-fits each wet race.
- Treat fuel correction (k_fuel=0.030 s/kg, Heilmeier consensus, range 0.019-0.036) as a fixed pre-processing step, not a fitted parameter, so k1 is conditional on it. At Bahrain's ~1.9 kg/lap burn a 0.010 s/kg error biases k1 by ~0.39 s of correction across a 20-lap stint. The self-consistency (same fuel model everywhere) is the defence; a k1-vs-fuel-model sensitivity sweep is an acknowledged gap, not yet run.


### CAUSAL IDENTIFICATION

- Identify the wake penalty from WITHIN-driver variation: per-(race,driver) fixed effects (calibrate.py:619-634) absorb each car's base pace, so theta is the marginal effect of closeness, never a slow-car-in-traffic level effect.
- Held-station filter (gap < 3.0s flag AND gap not closing faster than 0.4 s/lap) in fastf1_loader.py:189-193 to drop laps where a faster car is reeling in a slower one — the principal confounder. Threshold is a heuristic, not swept; acknowledged.
- Proximity regressor max(0, gap0 - gap_ahead) with gap0=2.5s, evaluated only on held laps, reported at a representative rep_gap=0.7s (calibrate.py:561,595,642).
- Single robust fixed-effects regression (IRLS-Huber, 4 passes, k=1.345) jointly estimating per-driver level, per-compound offset+age, per-race linear track evolution, and proximity — so track rubber-in and degradation cannot leak into theta (calibrate.py:627-640).
- Fit each circuit on its OWN three-season pool of 880-1516 held laps as six INDEPENDENT estimates (not one pooled regression); the agreement of those six with aero physics is the validation.
- Ship-gate discipline: write dirty_air_loss_s only if theta>0 AND n_held>=200 (build_priors.py:85). A wrong-signed or thin fit is dropped, same non-ship discipline as the inert temperature coefficient. Hard clamp min(loss, 1.2) caps a pathological fit (calibrate.py:647).
- Deploy as a STEP penalty: the sim adds the single calibrated loss whenever on-track gap < dirty_air_gap=1.0s (race.py:237-238,253-254), not the continuous linear ramp — a deliberate speed/discretisation simplification, with the deployment trigger (1.0s) deliberately distinct from the fit window (2.5s).
- Persist only dirty_air_loss_s and dirty_air_n in priors.json; theta and r are computed but intentionally not stored — a real gap to close if per-circuit fit statistics are wanted downstream.


### SIMULATION & OPTIMISATION

- Two-stage optimiser. Stage 1 (deterministic.py) is an EXACT shortest-path DP over stint boundaries -- it enumerates all predecessors, not a greedy choice, so it is optimal for any additive stint-cost table (convexity is interpretive, not required for correctness). It generates a free-track shortlist in milliseconds. Stage 2 (robust.py) re-scores the shortlist under the full stochastic MC model. The shortlist is deliberately diversified by stop count (best 1/2/3-stop guaranteed) so a track-position 1-stop is never pruned before the robust pass sees it.
- Common random numbers. The SC/VSC/RED per-lap timeline AND the per-scenario noise/retirement seed are sampled once into a ScenarioSet and replayed identically across every candidate, so strategy comparisons have high positive covariance and the difference variance collapses. This is the single highest-leverage choice for a reliable optimiser -- but it reduces variance on the COMPARISON only, not bias on the absolute finish.
- Rust accelerator trades RNG-stream fidelity for ~60x throughput and is validated DISTRIBUTIONALLY, not bit-for-bit: mean position within 0.25 and podium within 0.05 over 1500 scenarios on one configuration. Pure-Python is the declared source of truth for any policy decision; Rust is a batch screener. The same thermal-aware k1 slope is passed into Rust so the two cannot silently diverge on the tyre model.
- Live re-optimiser (live_optimizer.reoptimize) is a DETERMINISTIC free-track remaining-time minimisation via prefix sums, O(1) per plan, ranking by expected remaining green time with a cheap-pit window under SC/VSC. It is risk-NEUTRAL by design (no CVaR, no ensemble) to hit sub-millisecond latency; it reacts to the regime that has occurred and reruns every lap off the live Kalman belief rather than pricing unrealised future SCs. This is a conscious latency-vs-risk-awareness trade, not an oversight to hide.
- Overtaking carries 2019 TUM priors (p_overtake=0.25, threshold_s=1.8, duel_penalty=0.3) because the parameter is weakly identified from available telemetry; refitting on weak signal would overfit. Acknowledged as plausibly stale for the 2025 ground-effect aero regime. Backtest Spearman ~0.72 shows order survives the miscalibration, but the clear-air-vs-track-position risk profile is explicitly not claimed to be calibrated.
- Wet strategy fits only the absolute deficits (inter 9.23s, wet 18.54s) from 31 wet laps across 2 races, using each race's own dry laps as baseline; the wetness-response curvature stays a physical convex prior pending a real moisture signal. The DP consumes a wetness timeline that is a proxy partly derived from field tyre mix -- so matching VER's real switch laps is a behavioural sanity check with partial circularity, not an independent statistical validation.
- Tyre-set allocation constraint (DEFAULT_ALLOCATION 3S/2M/2H; ghost infers a per-race allocation capped at FIA 8S/3M/2H) is enforced in enumeration, the live sweep and the ghost policy, so every proposed plan is set-feasible. Two-compound legality is enforced separately and relaxed only for a car that legitimately retired before completing its second compound.
- Ghost counterfactual forces the SAME real retirement lap in BOTH the real-strategy and AI-policy runs under CRN, so a genuine DNF cancels and the ghost DELTA is net strategy under matched reliability. The +7-position Bahrain headline is the weaker sim-finish-vs-real-finish quantity (contaminated by sim absolute error and, if he finished, by a pathological 6-stop baseline); the matched ghost median delta is the honest number and is smaller. Report real and sim finish side by side, always.


### ML, LIVE & LATENCY

- Kalman process/measurement noise (R=0.09, Q=diag(1e-3, 5e-5)) hand-set and frozen rather than per-stint fitted — with only ~10-20 laps/stint, (R,Q) are weakly identified and trade off against each other; conservative fixed values give graceful, validated recovery (planted b=0.08 recovered to 0.02). Offline EM-fit of Q is the upgrade once a full season of stints is available.
- Robust soft-gating (inflate R_eff = R*(|z|/gate_z)^2 for |z|>4, keep the lap) rather than deleting traffic laps — avoids needing an outlier classifier, degrades continuously at the threshold, and self-heals (validated: planted +4s spike flagged and washed out by 3 laps later). Separate anomaly_z=3.0 only labels for the dashboard.
- Heteroscedastic surrogate (Gaussian-NLL log-variance head + MSE spread head) rather than a point predictor — gives a relative robustness signal for close pit-lap calls; treated as a relative confidence indicator, NOT a calibrated absolute CI (only +-1sigma coverage checked, no reliability diagram / tail / OOD calibration).
- Deterministic prefix-sum O(1)-per-plan reoptimize with the triangular-number identity for fresh-stint cost rather than naive nested loops — genuinely constant-time per plan, but gross enumeration is large (~11.6k evals/~14ms early race, ~5.8k/~7ms mid-race, pure Python); capped at max_more_stops=2 on purpose (3-stops-from-here would be O(n^3) and route to the DP optimiser instead).
- Surrogate trained on random strategies across random contexts with focal pace sampled 50% INDEPENDENT of grid — deliberately covers off-diagonal what-if cases (fast car from the back); wet/INTERMEDIATE excluded entirely (one-hot to zeros) and routed to the dedicated wet DP engine, not silently emulated.
- Two-component live state [fresh_pace, deg_rate] off lap time only rather than a temp/fuel-augmented state — stays interpretable and O(1)/lap, at the cost of early-stint precision and thermal nonlinearity; mitigated by the pit_advisor fresh_pace/fresh_deg override (hybrid human+ML), and bounded by the fact real pit calls cluster at lap 20+ post-convergence.
- Bayesian pit window by pushing 400 posterior (a,b) draws through the nonlinear argmin optimiser rather than a closed-form mean+-z*sigma — correctly propagates the a-b correlation and the non-Gaussian, discrete pit-lap distribution; fixed seed for reproducibility, PSD-failure fallback to point estimate.
- README one-step-ahead RMSPE corrected from a single favourable stint (0.37s) to the honest field aggregate (Kalman ~0.78 / OLS ~0.84 / persistence ~0.69) — Kalman's claim is now 'beats OLS, matches persistence one-step, and uniquely provides the multi-lap forecast + covariance + anomaly score persistence cannot', not 'beats all baselines'.
- Temperature->degradation coupling shipped INERT (temp_coeff=0.0) rather than forcing a wrong-signed S/M/H-aggregated slope — reactivates with per-compound Pirelli construction + tyre-surface temps; honest-zero over a wrong sign.


### F1 Strategy Domain Realism

- SC/VSC cheap-stop discount (sc_saving_frac=0.50, vsc=0.35) is a published prior applied in live_optimizer.pit_cost within a 3-lap window — deliberately NOT fitted, because PitOut−PitIn telemetry can't separate the SC saving from crew-speed/double-stack/geometry confounds, so a fitted number would be spurious. Re-decided every lap, so a sub-optimal cheap stop is corrected next lap.
- Undercut/overcut (_battle) is a DETERMINISTIC time-delta off the Kalman belief (per_lap_gain × laps_out − gap), reported as a directional decision-support signal, NOT a probability. It has no ground-truth test and no error bar; the 0.13–0.29 r-values belong to a different module (dirty-air calibration) and must not be presented as battle-prediction accuracy.
- Wet-strategy switch-lap 'validation' (Zandvoort L62 / Monaco 2023 L56) is RETRACTED as a claim — no test asserts it; only synthetic-timeline DP tests exist. The DP is structurally correct and the absolute deficits are calibrated, but curvature/optima are priors and the pool is ~2 races/~31 laps. Status: structurally built, NOT validated to real switch laps.
- Ghost allocation cap (_infer_allocation: field-max +1 per compound, capped at FIA 8/3/2) is enforced as a HARD alloc_ok constraint inside the live policy (selection-time), and feasibility is reported post-hoc as within_allocation AND two_compound SEPARATELY. It is a count constraint, not a tyre-lifespan/condition guarantee — no FP-mileage model exists.
- Pit event is modelled as single-lap cost + 1.0s cold out-lap penalty. Pit-lane egress position, out-lap DRS suppression, dynamic double-stacking (double_stack_penalty=2.5s exists but is NOT applied), and pit-SEQUENCE order are all unmodelled — accepted as a scoping decision (strategy-space explorer, not a real-time release/sequence optimiser).
- Temperature→degradation coupling (temp_coeff) is shipped INERT at 0.0 — an honest non-ship, because the S/M/H-aggregated calibration gave a wrong-signed, insignificant slope (r≈−0.55). The end-to-end plumbing exists (per-lap weather join, slope(), evaluated at race track-temp in the ghost) and reactivates with Pirelli per-compound construction + tyre-surface temps.
- Live Kalman tyre filter is LINEAR (a + b·age) with a 0.8 deg-damp and prior-carry on pit reset; its 90% credible band is Gaussian/no-cliff. It deliberately omits a thermal-cliff term — the band understates risk during a cliff, which is conceded openly rather than papered over (k2 curvature lives only in the offline param model, not the live filter).
- The Norris +7 ghost headline is a strategy+reliability INTERACTION, not pure strategy: both real and AI runs inherit the real DNF cutoff via retire_of, so the AI can't un-break the car. The code exposes feasible/two_compound/retirement flags for filtering to clean comparisons, but does NOT auto-decompose the two effects — so the headline is caveated to clean races only.


### Software Engineering & Systems

- Pure-Python sim as source of truth + Rust/PyO3 accelerator: chosen for legibility/debuggability of the domain logic plus ~60x speed on the Monte-Carlo path (distinct from the surrogate's ~1.1M strategies/s). Parity is statistical-within-MC-error (test_native.py: 1500 scenarios, <0.25 positions, <0.05 p_podium), not bit-for-bit, because RNG streams differ. Automatic Python fallback when _native fails to import.
- FEED seam (LiveStrategist.ingest dict): genuinely decouples the engine from the data source — Kalman/advisor/reoptimizer are source-blind. KNOWN GAP: stream_events does not actually pull live OpenF1 laps; it detects a live session only to set a label and replays the historical FastF1 race. The live feed is a small, bounded job thanks to the seam, but it is not built.
- lru_cache (12 in server.py): correct for a what-if tool (keyed by circuit/grid/delta), but a stale-data trap once data changes under a running process. load_priors() self-clears in save_priors(), but _model()/_surrogate() cache downstream artifacts and never invalidate; there is no /api/reload. Fix: shadow-cache rebuild + atomic swap, keyed by a priors content-hash (priors.json currently has no build timestamp/hash).
- Calibrated priors as committed JSON (build_priors.py): trades live freshness for reproducibility (deterministic IRLS-Huber, RMSE ~0.52s) and per-circuit auditability (n_races, p_sc_raw, pit_by_team stored). Provenance gap: meta block has source/seasons but no build timestamp or content hash of the input data, so reproducibility is only conditional on the upstream FastF1 snapshot.
- Surrogate as search prefilter, Monte-Carlo as arbiter: heatmap (surrogate, ~0.08ms for 47 cells) explores; robust_optimize scores the shortlist with the real sim. CORRECTION vs prior draft: R²=0.93 / mean-error-1.0 are train.py metrics quoted in the README, NOT test assertions — test_surrogate.py only checks shapes/legality. The surrogate's top-N rank vs MC and its tail accuracy are unvalidated; needs a Spearman rank-correlation regression test in CI.
- Live-loop latency profiled, not assumed: per car ~55ms worst case — Kalman ~1ms, reoptimize 7.5k-13.5k plans in 8-16ms (NOT O(1) overall; per-stint cost is O(1) via prefix sums, the sweep is O(n^2*|C|^2)), pit_advisor.advise(n_samples=400) ~37ms median (the dominant cost), surrogate forward ~0.07ms (a non-issue). field_model runs all 20 drivers sequentially (no threading/async/pool), so full-field is ~1s. Architecture: pre-warm caches, vectorise/shrink the 400-sample window, process-shard by driver (GIL means threads won't parallelise CPU-bound Python), react under SC with reoptimize-only and defer the Bayesian window.
- Test suite (43 tests, 552 lines) covers synthetic calibration/Kalman recovery, native parity, model geometry, optimisation constraints; network tests DO assert real calibration deg-bands and backtest Spearman>0.5 but are gated off (PITWALL_NETWORK_TESTS=1) and excluded from CI. Under-tested: surrogate accuracy/rank regression, end-to-end live replay + scorecard, Kalman robustness under traffic, and data-failure/idempotency paths.
- Ghost counterfactual: focal real-vs-AI under the real SC timeline with CRN against a FROZEN rival field (real strategies, forced real retirements). Honest upper bound on strategic gain — frozen rivals can't cover the AI's undercut, and the single ghost can't decompose SC-timing luck from strategic skill. Missing ablations (SC-disabled, rivals-also-AI, nominal field) are the fix; field_model already has the all-20-cars machinery. Trust the real-vs-AI delta, not the absolute sim finish (few-positions absolute error).

## Full limitations register

Every limitation surfaced during the review, by area, for completeness.


### STATISTICS & CALIBRATION

- **Temperature->degradation coupling fitted but shipped inert (temp_coeff=0.0). The two-stage S/M/H fit returned r=-0.553 over 45 (compound,race) points - wrong-signed vs the physical prior. The model is therefore insensitive to track-temperature variation in wear.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Shipping a wrong-signed coefficient is strictly worse than zero: it would steepen wear on cool tracks and flatten it on hot ones, inverting reality. The confounds are real and unbreakable at this resolution - Pirelli re-specs compounds year-to-year so a 2023 'Soft' isn't a 2024 'Soft', and setup/fuel correlate with ambient. The plumbing (slope(), native-consistent) is live, so reactivation is a one-line coefficient swap, not a rebuild. Reported honestly in priors.json (r=-0.553, shipped=0.0).
  - *Fixed by:* Per-compound thermal-construction data from Pirelli; per-lap tyre-surface-temperature telemetry to regress wear on actual rubber temp rather than air/track proxy; fitting at the individual-compound level within a single construction year rather than pooling S/M/H across re-specs.
- **SC/VSC cheap-stop discount is the published prior (0.50/0.35), not a shipped data fit - even though calibrate_pit_loss computes a telemetry fraction. The build script intentionally discards that fraction.**
  - *Acceptable because:* PitOut-PitIn measures pit-lane transit, which barely changes under SC; the time *saved* is time the field loses while crawling, which PitOut-PitIn cannot see. The computed fraction is therefore not the estimand and was honestly rejected (comment in build_circuit). The 0.50/0.35 figures are well-established and the discount is applied to the mean only, so it can't manufacture a free stop. This is the single biggest unverified lever on under/overcut value and I flag it as such.
  - *Fixed by:* Field-synchronous gap reconstruction (all 20 cars on a common clock) to measure the actual delta between a neutralised stop and the same car's unstopped counterfactual; or a ghost-style sim estimate of time-lost-to-field under SC vs green, validated against a handful of real neutralised stops.
- **Full-wet pace floor (18.54 s) rests on 31 full-wet laps across 2 races (Zandvoort 2023, Monaco 2023); both tactical/transitional rather than steady-state full-wet. Wide credible interval. (The intermediate floor at 9.23 s is on firmer ground - 471 inter laps.)**
  - *Acceptable because:* Each race's own dry baseline cancels circuit/setup, and the model's *ordering* validates externally - the wet DP calls VER's real inter switch at Zandvoort L62 and Monaco 2023 L56. The thin-data risk is concentrated in the full-wet floor specifically, and full-wet running is rare in modern F1 anyway. Sample sizes are surfaced (n_wet=31) so a strategist never over-trusts the magnitude, and the live layer re-fits each wet race.
  - *Fixed by:* Accumulating future full-wet races (1-3 globally per season); back-extending FastF1 coverage to older wet races; or weighting the full-wet floor toward the better-sampled intermediate floor via the physical bowl until more full-wet data exists.
- **Dirty-air proximity model has modest partial r-values (roughly 0.13-0.29), so the linear wake term explains only ~10-20% of held-station lap variance. The point estimate per circuit is noisy.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Wake depends on unobserved aero setup and driver inputs (steering, lift-and-coast, DRS) that aren't in the feature set, so low r is expected, not a bug. What matters for strategy is the sign and the cross-circuit ordering, and both are physically correct (Monaco 0.83 >> Monza 0.13) on large held-lap counts (880-1500). The ship gate (theta>0 and n_held>=200) blocks spurious fits, and the value only moves under/overcut economics, not safety-critical calls.
  - *Fixed by:* Adding controllable covariates (DRS state, throttle/brake traces, steering) to regress out driver-input variance; CFD-derived per-setup wake to decouple baseline from proximity; more races per circuit to tighten the proximity coefficient.
- **k1 degradation slopes are conditional on the fixed fuel model (k_fuel=0.030 s/kg). A true 0.020 or 0.040 would bias k1, and no k1-vs-fuel-model sensitivity sweep has been run.**
  - *Acceptable because:* 0.030 s/kg is the published consensus within a tight range (0.019-0.036), and crucially the same fuel model is used in calibration, simulation and ghost, so the engine is internally self-consistent - the bias largely cancels in strategy *deltas*, which is the quantity decisions are made on. The residual risk is in absolute k1, and the per-event re-fit re-derives k1 under whatever fuel model is set.
  - *Fixed by:* A sensitivity sweep re-fitting k1 at k_fuel in {0.020, 0.030, 0.040} and reporting the k1 band (cheap, not yet done); per-lap fuel-mass telemetry if a team provides it, replacing the linear burn assumption entirely.
- **Multi-year pooling (2023-25) assumes Pirelli compound construction is stable across the window; a mid-window re-spec would blend two physically different tyres under one label.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Construction changes are infrequent (every 2-3 years) and announced, and 3 years is the minimum that gives enough compound overlap to pin slopes while staying short enough that per-year k1 drift is visible as a diagnostic. The build is re-runnable, so dropping or down-weighting an obsoleted year is a config change.
  - *Fixed by:* Per-year separate fits with a recency-weighted blend; Pirelli spec sheets to gate which years share a construction; an explicit change-point test on year-over-year k1 to auto-detect a re-spec.


### CAUSAL IDENTIFICATION

- **The held-station filter never verifies the car ahead is the SAME identity across consecutive laps; the closing-rate delta compares gap-to-whoever-is-ahead this lap vs last lap (fastf1_loader.py:185-193), so pit cycles, retirements and overtakes ahead corrupt a minority of held flags.**
  - *Acceptable because:* A changed car-ahead usually produces a large gap jump that pushes the lap out of the <3.0s window anyway, and the residual misclassification is regressor noise that attenuates theta toward zero (conservative, not inflationary). With ~1000 held laps per circuit a few percent of bad laps is tolerable.
  - *Fixed by:* Carry the car-ahead driver ID per lap and require the same ID across the consecutive laps used for the closing-rate test, ideally requiring >=3 consecutive held laps behind the same car before counting the proximity term.
- **theta and the diagnostic r are computed at fit time but NOT persisted; priors.json stores only dirty_air_loss_s and dirty_air_n (build_priors.py:86-87). Quoted r=0.13-0.29 is observed-at-fit, not readable from the shipped store, and is a raw (not partial) correlation diluted by the many clean-air laps with prox=0.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The shipped quantity (the loss) is what the sim consumes; the build gate enforces theta>0 and n_held>=200 so a non-positive or thin fit never ships, which is the load-bearing quality check, not r. The raw r is conservative, so it understates rather than oversells fit quality.
  - *Fixed by:* Persist theta, a partial r against the post-fixed-effects residual, and a bootstrap standard error per circuit in priors.json (a ~10-line change to calibrate_dirty_air's return plumbing and build_priors).
- **No sensitivity sweep over the held thresholds (gap0, 3.0s window, 0.4 s/lap closing rate) and no bootstrap/credible interval anywhere in the calibration code; the loss is a bare point estimate.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The proximity window (gap0=2.5s) sitting inside the held flag (3.0s) makes the estimate insensitive to the held cutoff in that band, and the cross-circuit ordering is driven by aero regime so it should survive any reasonable threshold choice. The downstream strategy decision uses only the coarse magnitude, which is robust at both ends (Monaco vs Monza).
  - *Fixed by:* Grid search over (gap0, closing_rate) reporting theta and a bootstrap SE per circuit; replace the fixed 0.4 s/lap closing rate with a per-circuit value from the gap-change distribution or actual telemetry closing speed.
- **The fit is linear in closeness but the sim applies a flat STEP penalty (the loss at rep_gap=0.7s) for any gap < 1.0s (race.py:237-238,253-254); the deployment trigger gap (1.0s) differs from the calibration window (2.5s), and a car at 0.95s and 0.1s get the identical tax.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The sim's track-position model already discretises following into in-window / not, the track-position clamp dominates the dynamics, and rep_gap=0.7s lies inside the 1.0s trigger so the shipped magnitude matches a gap the sim actually sees. min(loss,1.2) caps any pathological value.
  - *Fixed by:* Make the sim honor the continuous theta*max(0, gap0 - gap) ramp instead of a step (a small change in race.py), so fine gap-level decisions reflect the fitted law.
- **Per-(race,driver) fixed effects absorb only the LEVEL of pace, not the per-driver slope in traffic, so theta is a population-average wake penalty; and tyre-management lifting in a train is indistinguishable from aero wake and biases theta upward.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The strategy engine needs a circuit-level average penalty for schedule optimisation, for which the population-average is the right object; the tyre-management bias is partially offset by gap-measurement attenuation, and the decision the number feeds (pit-for-clear-air) is robust to the resulting band.
  - *Fixed by:* A hierarchical/random-effects specification giving each driver a wake slope (needs ~50-100 held laps per driver in traffic, marginal at the current three-race pool), and conditioning on throttle/lift telemetry to separate deliberate backing-off from forced wake loss.
- **Each circuit is fit on its own pool of held laps reconstructed from per-lap Position+Time snapshots, which can briefly misorder the field around safety-car restarts and lapped traffic; restart green out-laps can slip in as legitimately-close green laps.**
  - *Acceptable because:* SC/VSC laps themselves are excluded upstream (TrackStatus=='1', IsAccurate, in load_clean_laps), lapped cars are too slow to register as held, and ~1000 held laps per circuit is robust to a handful of misclassified points; the six independent estimates still reproduce the aero ordering.
  - *Fixed by:* Drop N laps after every green flag to exclude restart bunching, and use continuous millisecond-resolution position from the live-timing feed rather than per-lap line-crossing snapshots to eliminate snapshot misordering.


### SIMULATION & OPTIMISATION

- **The shipped tyre calibration is effectively LINEAR: fit_compounds_pooled sets k2=0 and fit_compounds only fits k2 when quadratic=True (not used by default). The quadratic cliff term in CompoundParams (~0.0003-0.0011 s/lap^2) is a hand-set prior, not calibrated, so a real tyre cliff is not captured by the fit.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The exact DP is optimal for any cost table regardless of curvature, so plans are still well-posed; the prior k2 is small and physically reasonable; and a real cliff surfaces LIVE as rising degradation in the Kalman belief, pulling the stop forward, rather than being committed at the deterministic stage.
  - *Fixed by:* More high-stint-length laps per compound to identify k2 cleanly (multi-year pooling already exists), or Pirelli construction data to set curvature per compound, then enable quadratic=True with a proper out-of-sample check that the cliff is not just fitting fuel/track-evolution residual.
- **Temperature->degradation coupling is built end-to-end but held inert (temp_coeff=0.0). The S/M/H-aggregated fit gave a wrong-signed, insignificant slope (r=-0.55).**
  - *Acceptable because:* Held inert rather than shipped mis-specified; a 5-10C track-temp swing is ~0.01-0.02 s/lap against a 0.5s RMSE noise floor, so defaulting to zero is safer than injecting an aggregate-fit artefact. The thermal-aware slope plumbing is consistent across Python, Rust and the live engine, so reactivation is a one-parameter change.
  - *Fixed by:* Per-compound construction data plus tyre-surface temperature telemetry, then a within-compound thermal sensitivity refit at a reference temp (calibrate_temp_coupling already structures this as a two-stage estimator); ship only if the slope is correctly signed and significant.
- **SC pit-saving fraction (sc_saving_frac=0.50, vsc 0.35) is a research prior, not recoverable from PitOut-PitIn telemetry, which measures pit-lane time, not time gained versus the neutralised field.**
  - *Acceptable because:* 50% of a 22s loss (~11s gain) under SC is empirically in the right range -- the field compresses and there is no traffic on exit -- and the backtest does not blow up, so it is the right ballpark even if not exact. It is a single scalar, easy to sensitivity-test.
  - *Fixed by:* Build a dataset of (entry gap to field, pit loss, post-pit gap, regime) from real neutralised stops and fit the effective saving directly against the field, separating SC from VSC.
- **Overtaking constants (p_overtake=0.25, threshold_s=1.8, duel_penalty=0.3) are uncalibrated 2019 TUM values from a different aero era; closer 2025 following plausibly makes them stale in a known direction.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Refitting needs lap-by-lap duel reconstruction (was there a pace advantage; was a pass attempted) which is labour-intensive and noisy; a credible conservative prior beats overfitting. Backtest Spearman ~0.72 shows pecking-order recovery, and if p_overtake is really higher, the bias is to under-value undercuts -- a directional, non-fatal error I can reason about.
  - *Fixed by:* A season of FIA position-delta + DRS-status logs (plus machine-vision attempt detection) to fit p_overtake and threshold_s per circuit and per aero era.
- **Wet calibration is sparse (31 laps, 2 races) and only the absolute deficits are fitted; curvature is a prior. The wetness timeline is a proxy partly derived from the field's own tyre mix, making the 'called VER's switch laps' validation partly circular.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Wets are genuinely rare so the data ceiling is structural; the model degrades gracefully (a 10% curvature error still picks a sensible crossover), and the deficits are tyre-count-weighted across drivers. The honest claim is narrow: given a wetness curve the DP finds the crossover.
  - *Fixed by:* An exogenous wetness signal (trackside moisture sensors / tyre-surface temps) to break the circularity and identify curvature, plus more wet/mixed races pooled over many seasons.
- **Dirty-air wake loss has modest fit quality (r 0.13-0.29). Causality is confounded -- a slow car ahead may be slow because it is on old tyres, not because of its wake.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The fit is reported honestly with r and n_held; the held-station filter (within a gap window, not closing >0.4s/lap) already excludes a fast car catching a slow one; the recovered ordering is physically right (Monaco ~0.83s >> Monza ~0.13s). Where r is low, age/track-evolution swamp the wake term and CVaR risk-adjustment absorbs the residual uncertainty.
  - *Fixed by:* A tighter held-in-wake dataset (gap held in a narrow band for 3+ laps, no strategy change), ideally first-stint-only to fix tyre age, isolating wake from age/evolution confounds.
- **Backtest Spearman 0.72 is a single aggregate, and it is CONTAMINATED: real DNFs are scored position 99 on the actual side but run to the flag in the simulation (the sim does not force real retirements -- that is the ghost's job), so reliability mismatch depresses the number for reasons unrelated to strategy. There is also no per-circuit/condition breakdown.**
  - *Acceptable because:* It is a smoke test that order is recovered, and the DNF contamination only makes 0.72 a conservative FLOOR on pure-strategy fidelity, not an overstatement. Podium accuracy is the cleaner top-line metric I lean on.
  - *Fixed by:* Right-censor or exclude real DNFs (or force them under CRN), report Spearman on classified finishers only, and break results out per circuit/year/weather with MAE, podium accuracy and bootstrap CIs to expose where the model is systematically wrong.
- **Production MC is typically 300-500 scenarios; MC error is ~few percent on mean position and ~3-5% on p_podium, so a true 0.1-position difference can be swamped by sampling luck.**
  - *Acceptable because:* CRN attacks exactly this by sharing scenarios across candidates, so rank-ordering is stable at modest n even when the absolute estimates are noisy; live calls refresh every lap so per-lap noise averages out over the decision sequence.
  - *Fixed by:* Raise n to 1000-2000 on the shortlist where compute allows, add importance sampling toward SC/tail scenarios, and report the comparison's CRN-paired standard error so a strategist sees when two plans are statistically indistinguishable.
- **The Rust parity test gates only mean position and podium probability on one circuit/grid configuration; it does not test the full position distribution or CVaR, which is the quantity the robust optimiser sells.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Mean+podium catches gross logic divergence and the same model parameters (including thermal-aware k1) are fed to both engines, so they share the simulator by construction. Rust is confined to mean/probability screening; the shortlist tail is re-run in Python.
  - *Fixed by:* Add a two-sample KS test on the position array and a CVaR-delta assertion across several circuits and grid/pace configs before trusting Rust for any tail-sensitive output.


### ML, LIVE & LATENCY

- **On the proper one-step-ahead benchmark across a full field (Bahrain 2023, ~24-28 stints) the Kalman beats growing-window OLS (~0.78 vs ~0.84s RMSPE) but loses to naive persistence (~0.69s); the README's 0.37s was a single clean stint, not the aggregate.**
  - *Acceptable because:* One-step-ahead RMSPE is the wrong yardstick for this component: persistence ('next lap = this lap') is unbeatable at one step because lap-to-lap degradation (~0.05-0.1s) is below the 0.3s noise floor, but persistence cannot forecast multiple laps, emit a covariance, or flag anomalies — which is the entire job (forecast_stint + pit_advisor window + anomaly score). The Kalman's real win is over OLS, the relevant online-fit competitor.
  - *Fixed by:* Report the field aggregate in the README; add a multi-lap-horizon (e.g. 10-lap-ahead) benchmark where flat persistence extrapolation diverges and the Kalman forecast should dominate; publish the credible-band coverage on real stints to substantiate the uncertainty claim.
- **Live state [fresh_pace, deg_rate] ignores tyre temperature, fuel beyond the global model, and track surface; converges in ~6-8 laps so pit decisions before ~lap 8-10 have over-wide bands, and the optimiser mixes a LIVE current-stint slope with a STATIC fitted fresh-stint slope (no mid-race refit), biasing which stint it wants to extend.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Real pit calls cluster at lap 20+ where the filter has converged and temps are steady; early SC calls use practice-session fresh-pace priors via the pit_advisor override (hybrid human+ML) rather than the raw live belief. The live/static slope mix is bounded by robust gating.
  - *Fixed by:* Reactivate temp->deg coupling (per-compound Pirelli construction + tyre-surface temps) so the H matrix carries temperature as an exogenous; refit/thermally-adjust the fresh-stint slope mid-race; or move to a particle/UKF tyre-temp hidden-state model.
- **Surrogate heteroscedastic head is validated only by +-1sigma coverage (a single scalar near ~0.68), with no reliability diagram, no tail calibration, and no out-of-distribution coverage check — so it could be over-confident in the tails without detection.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The surrogate is used for triage (sweeping the pit-lap x compound heatmap interactively), not as the final trigger: the top candidates are re-run through the Rust Monte-Carlo before any commit, so a mis-calibrated surrogate tail cannot directly fire an irreversible call.
  - *Fixed by:* Full calibration: reliability diagram + per-quantile coverage on held-out and OOD slices; temperature-scale or recalibrate the variance head; report sharpness alongside coverage so a wide-but-covering band is not mistaken for a good one.
- **Surrogate has zero wet coverage (INTERMEDIATE/WET one-hot to all-zeros, dataset samples slicks only) and feature sampling tops out at focal_delta ~2.4 (normaliser 3.0), so a +2.5s pace gap is edge-extrapolation and a wet query degrades SILENTLY — there is no OOD gate.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Wet is handled by the dedicated wet DP engine (optimal_wet_strategy), not the surrogate, and dry 1-2 stop strategies cover the large majority of decisions; the off-diagonal pace sampling already covers most fast-car-from-the-back cases up to ~2.4s.
  - *Fixed by:* Add a Mahalanobis-distance OOD gate on the feature vector that routes off-manifold queries to the DP/MC path; widen the focal_delta sampling range past 2.5s; generate wet training data via the wet physics engine and a wet head, or keep wet strictly on the DP path with an explicit guard.
- **reoptimize gross enumeration is large in pure Python (~11.6k evals/~14ms early race; n_eval counts pre-gate so it overstates kept plans) and is hard-capped at max_more_stops=2; a 3-stops-from-here scenario is not swept.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Tens of milliseconds is well within a once-per-lap live budget, and O(1)-per-plan with closed-form fresh-stint cost keeps it flat per plan; 3-stops-from-current-state is rare and would add an O(n^3) nest, so routing it to the DP optimiser is the right trade.
  - *Fixed by:* Vectorise the inner sweep in NumPy or push it into the Rust accelerator if a tighter budget is needed; report n_eval as kept-plans rather than gross enumeration; add an explicit DP fallback path for >=3 remaining stops.
- **Feature normalisers in the surrogate carry a redundant hardcoded pre-divide (n_laps/70, pit_loss/30, focal_delta/3) on top of the learned standardiser (set_norm x_mean/x_std); the pre-divide adds no modelling value and a 70-lap / >35s-pit-loss circuit lands in the standardiser tails and extrapolates poorly.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Every circuit in the current pool sits within ~50-60 laps and ~20-28s pit loss, so the issue never binds in practice; the learned standardiser absorbs a linear pre-divide mathematically, so predictions on in-distribution circuits are unaffected.
  - *Fixed by:* Drop the hardcoded divisors and let one StandardScaler (already present via set_norm) own all normalisation; refit when the circuit pool changes; pair with the OOD gate so a new long-lap / high-pit-loss circuit flags instead of silently extrapolating.
- **Robust gating uses an unbounded (|z|/gate_z)^2 R-inflation and a separate hard anomaly flag at z=3.0; a single extreme garbage lap still nudges the state (influence ~1/z^2 but never exactly zero) and there is no redescending weight.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Influence falls off as 1/z^2 so extreme outliers contribute negligibly, and the validated self-heal (planted +4s spike washed out within 3 laps) shows the filter is not derailed in practice; the soft down-weight is deliberately chosen over deletion to avoid an outlier classifier.
  - *Fixed by:* Add a redescending (Tukey biweight) weight or a hard cap on R_eff so beyond a chosen z the lap contributes exactly zero; unify the gate and anomaly thresholds so the labelled-anomaly and down-weighted-lap sets are consistent.


### F1 Strategy Domain Realism

- **The undercut/overcut engine (_battle) is a deterministic margin off the Kalman belief with no error bar and no ground-truth test against real undercut outcomes. The only r-values in scope (0.13–0.29) are from the separate dirty-air proximity fit, NOT from _battle, so attaching them to undercut accuracy would be a category error.**
  - *Acceptable because:* It captures direction and order-of-magnitude off validated inputs (Kalman beats persistence/OLS on lap-time RMSPE; the full sim hits Spearman 0.72), and real strategists also act on directional heuristics. For COMPARATIVE plan selection the relative delta matters more than an absolute calibrated margin, and the live reoptimiser — not _battle — makes the actual pit call.
  - *Fixed by:* Add a sim-sensitivity band: vary rival pace within the Kalman covariance and report 'undercut +2.1 ±1.5 positions'. Then back-test flagged-viable undercuts against real position swaps across a season and publish hit-rate, so the signal carries an empirical accuracy.
- **Wet-strategy switch laps are NOT validated to real races — no test asserts the Zandvoort/Monaco lap numbers; the claim was build-note over-reach. Calibration covers ~2 wet races / ~31 laps for the absolute deficits, and the response curvature/optima are unfitted physical priors.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Wet races are rare (~2/season) so clean data is structurally scarce, and the DP itself is provably optimal given the timeline and deficits. The dry engine — ~95% of operational use — carries real backtest validation (Spearman 0.72). The honest framing is 'structurally sound, pilot-tested on synthetic timelines'.
  - *Fixed by:* Run analyze_wet_race over historical wet races and ASSERT optimal_wet_strategy's switch laps match top finishers' real stops within ±2 laps; report accuracy. Fit the wetness-response curvature once track-wetness-sensor or per-compound wet data is available.
- **The SC/VSC cheap-stop discount (50%/35%) is an unfitted published prior and the engine does not forecast SC DURATION — it reacts to the observed regime. A short SC (ends a lap after boxing) can leave the AI having spent its window while a waiter catches a later RED-flag free change.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The 50% saving can't be cleanly fitted from public PitOut−PitIn data (confounded by crew speed, double-stacking, geometry), so an honest prior beats spurious precision. The live engine re-optimises every lap, so a mistimed cheap stop is corrected next lap and the EV of an 11s-vs-22s saving dominates a few seconds of timing error. The ghost replays the REAL timeline, so the AI never gets clairvoyant SC knowledge.
  - *Fixed by:* Build a probabilistic SC-duration model (from historical neutralisation lengths per circuit) so the cheap-stop EV accounts for early-end risk, and fit per-team SC pit-loss from a telemetry database that tags stops by neutralisation state.
- **Pit-lane micro-modelling is absent: egress track position, out-lap DRS suppression for a freshly-pitted car, dynamic double-stacking (double_stack_penalty=2.5s is declared but never applied), and pit-sequence order are all unmodelled. The allocation cap is also a count constraint, not a tyre-lifespan check (no FP-mileage model), so a count-feasible plan can still be physically undriveable on stint length.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Pit-sequence/release optimisation is a real-time tactical layer orthogonal to strategic (lap, compound) selection; for comparing plans at equal grid/pace these effects are second-order. The k2 cliff-curvature in the objective already penalises over-long stints so undriveable plans rarely win the optimisation.
  - *Fixed by:* Wire up the existing double_stack_penalty to fire on concurrent same-lap stops, suppress DRS on the out-lap, add a pit-sequence module (bunching/egress), and add FP tyre-mileage tracking plus a max-stint-length cap so allocation reflects lifespan, not just set counts.
- **Temperature–degradation coupling is architected end-to-end but shipped INERT (temp_coeff=0.0): the S/M/H-aggregated calibration returned a wrong-signed, insignificant slope (r≈−0.55). The AI therefore does not shorten stints on a hot day. The live Kalman filter is also strictly linear and carries no thermal-cliff term, so its 90% band understates risk precisely during a cliff.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Shipping inert is correct: a wrong-signed temp term would flip strategy under false physics, which is worse than not modelling it. The plumbing (per-lap weather join, slope(), evaluation at race track-temp in the ghost) is ready, and the Kalman's anomaly flag (innovation_z>3) gives a coarse cliff tripwire even without a cliff term.
  - *Fixed by:* Re-fit temp_coeff PER COMPOUND (not S/M/H-aggregated) using Pirelli construction thermal sensitivity and FP runs across the track-temp range, validate the sign, then reactivate. Add a non-linear/changepoint term (or tyre-surface-temp input) to the live filter so cliffs aren't mis-specified.
- **The Norris +7 ghost headline conflates pure pit-strategy gain with a mechanical-DNF interaction (his real 6-stop was failure-forced). Both runs inherit the real retirement cutoff, so the AI can't un-break the car — but the code does NOT auto-decompose 'positions from strategy' vs 'positions from avoiding the broken-car stops'. The absolute sim also carries a ~2.5-position error.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The ghost is honest by construction: it reports feasible, two_compound and the retirement flag, letting a strategist filter to clean comparisons, and under common random numbers the DELTA (not the absolute finish) is the apples-to-apples quantity. The risk is presentation, not the code.
  - *Fixed by:* Report the delta ONLY over races where both runs are clean (focal_retire is None AND two_compound True), with a bootstrap CI across scenarios, and label DNF-interaction cases separately as 'strategy + reliability' with a distinct, smaller pure-strategy figure.


### Software Engineering & Systems

- **stream_events is not a live feed: it detects an OpenF1 live session only to set a source label, then replays the historical FastF1 race regardless. There is no code path pulling live laps into ingest(), and ingest() assumes monotonic, one-shot laps (it mutates Kalman state in place), so it has no dedup/idempotency or re-process-on-correction for the out-of-order, revised data OpenF1 actually emits.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The source-agnostic seam (LiveStrategist.ingest dict) is real and is the hard part; the missing piece is one poller emitting the same dict plus an idempotency layer. As a portfolio piece the live pipeline is faithfully demonstrated end-to-end (SSE, wall-clock pacing, scorecard) without claiming a trackside feed that wasn't built.
  - *Fixed by:* Write an OpenF1 poller that emits the ingest() dict; add a dedup/versioning layer keyed by (driver, lap) that ignores replays and re-runs the Kalman/advisor on a corrected lap. Engine downstream is unchanged.
- **Rust/Python dual maintenance with an under-powered parity test: any run_once logic change must be hand-ported to Rust, and test_native.py guards only one fixture (Bahrain, one field, one strategy distribution, 1500 scenarios). A strategic divergence that stays inside the 0.25-position band passes silently.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The ~60x is needed for the live MC path; fallback to Python is automatic if _native is absent. The single guard catches gross numerical divergence, and a strategic divergence would also dent the backtest Spearman, giving a second (if coarse) net.
  - *Fixed by:* A property-style parity sweep across circuits, stop-counts, and SC/VSC/RED regimes asserting the MC-error band holds everywhere, run on every commit that touches run_once or the Rust loop.
- **lru_cache stale-data trap with no invalidation hook: _model()/_surrogate()/_scenarios() cache built artifacts downstream of the priors JSON; a mid-session recalibration or surrogate retrain is invisible until process restart. No /api/reload exists; the only cache_clear() in the project is for the priors JSON inside save_priors(). priors.json carries no build timestamp or content hash.**
  - *Acceptable because:* Within a single race the priors are effectively fixed and the caches are correct (keyed by circuit/grid/delta) — staleness only bites when data changes under a running process, which is the not-yet-built live-calibration case.
  - *Fixed by:* Stamp priors.json with a build timestamp + content hash; key _model()/_surrogate() on that hash so a recalibration auto-invalidates. Rebuild into a shadow cache off the hot path and atomically swap, so no in-flight request eats the cold start.
- **Surrogate accuracy is unguarded by tests: R²=0.93 and mean-error-1.0 are train.py metrics quoted in the README, not assertions; test_surrogate.py checks only shapes and legality. Top-N rank agreement with the Monte-Carlo and tail accuracy (finishes 18-20) are unvalidated, yet the heatmap is what a human reads on the wall.**
  - *Acceptable because:* The MC is the arbiter for the final recommendation, so a surrogate rank error can't directly set the shipped plan, and a gross drift would show as a mismatch between the heatmap and the MC reason in the UI.
  - *Fixed by:* Add a CI test that samples ~1000 random strategies, scores both surrogate and MC, and asserts Spearman >= 0.95 on the top slice (>= 0.90 overall); flag heatmap cells where surrogate and MC disagree by > 0.5 positions as 'unconfirmed'.
- **Live-loop cost is dominated by pit_advisor.advise(n_samples=400) at ~37ms median (a pure-Python re-solve of the remaining-time curve per draw), and field_model runs all 20 drivers sequentially (~1s full field) with no parallelism. The reoptimize docstring claims 'thousands of strategies in a millisecond' but the real sweep is 7.5k-13.5k plans in 8-16ms.**
  - *Acceptable because:* For a single focal car the worst-case ~55ms/lap is well inside a 90-second-per-lap budget; the sequential full-field pass is only needed for the multi-agent view, not the focal recommendation, and the per-car stacks are independent so it parallelises cleanly.
  - *Fixed by:* Vectorise advise()'s 400-sample curve over draws (or drop to ~100 samples) to reclaim ~30ms; process-shard the field by driver (processes, not threads — the GIL blocks CPU-bound Python parallelism); fix the docstring to the measured numbers.
- **Ghost counterfactual uses a frozen rival field (real strategies, forced real retirements) and exposes a single run — so it grants the AI undercuts the real pit wall would have covered, and cannot decompose safety-car-timing luck from strategic skill. The Bahrain +7 headline is further inflated by a broken real car (mechanical 6-stop).**
  - *Acceptable because:* Under CRN the real-vs-AI delta is a clean apples-to-apples comparison and is the question strategists care about ('would the AI have finished higher?'); it is honestly an optimistic upper bound on strategic gain, and the project already states the sim's few-position absolute error means only the delta should be trusted.
  - *Fixed by:* Add ablations to build_ghost: SC-disabled (real timeline forced green) to isolate SC value; rivals-also-AI / best-response (field_model already runs the live engine for all 20) so undercuts get covered; a nominal-field baseline to separate strategy from car pace. Return the decomposition (finish_ai, finish_ai_no_sc, sc_value_s) instead of one blended number.
